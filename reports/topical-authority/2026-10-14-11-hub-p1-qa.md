## P0 confirmed published legal content accuracy defects
File `_posts/2026-09-27-cac-hang-bang-lai-xe-may-tai-viet-nam.md`; source blob `1328f3a119820924f485751f5dbf03c11de5dd76`. Both GitHub source and Tavily live Extract agree.
1. Hạng A1 paragraph **and FAQ** say 16 years old qualifies for A1 licence. Per Law 36/2024/QH15 Article 59(1), 16 may operate qualifying *xe gắn máy*; A1/A/B1 licences are issued from 18.
2. Hạng B1 paragraph excludes all two-wheel motorcycles. Article 57(1)(c) includes vehicles under A1 in B1 scope, besides three-wheel motorcycles.
3. Under "Điều kiện thi và thủ tục", the article asserts motorcycle licences expire and require renewal; Article 57(5)(a) states A1/A/B1 licences do **not** have fixed expiry dates. Related expired-licence FAQ must be reviewed.
Legal source: https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/toan-van-luat-trat-tu-an-toan-giao-thong-duong-bo-119240909105718285.htm
Prior plus new evidence: `reports/topical-authority/2026-10-13-p0-legal-accuracy.md`.
