## PRICE POLICY

Do NOT hardcode broad market prices permanently into the content factory.

When an article needs current information about:

- rental prices
- vehicle prices
- used motorbike prices
- electric motorbike prices
- parts prices
- repair prices
- service fees

SEARCH CURRENT SOURCES before writing.

Use multiple reasonable current sources where possible.

Record:

- source
- access date
- location/context
- whether price is exact or approximate

Use wording such as:

- "mức giá tham khảo"
- "giá thường gặp"
- "giá có thể thay đổi"
- "cần xác nhận trực tiếp với đơn vị cung cấp"

when the price is market-based.

Do not invent a precise price when reliable current evidence is unavailable.
