## Choose the Right Type of Motorcycle for Your Needs

Choosing the right motorcycle will greatly determine your experience. Here are suggestions based on usage purposes:

### Manual Motorcycle - Most Popular Choice

Manual motorcycles (xe so) are the most popular choice for customers renting motorcycles in Hanoi. Advantages of manual motorcycles:

- **Easy to control:** Suitable for almost everyone, including beginners
- **Fuel efficient:** Low fuel consumption, suitable for long trips
- **Durable:** Few minor breakdowns, suitable for Old Quarter terrain

Popular manual motorcycle models at Nguyen Tu Motorcycle Rental:
- **Honda Wave:** Popular for its durability and ease of use
- **Yamaha Sirius:** Modern design, suitable for women

If you are visiting Hanoi for the first time and are not familiar with the traffic here, a manual motorcycle is a safe choice.

### Automatic Scooter - Convenient for City Riding

Automatic scooters (xe ga) offer comfort with an upright riding position, suitable for:

- Short trips within the city
- Those who want to travel lightly, with less hand fatigue
- Women or those with a small physique

Automatic scooter models you can consider:
- **Honda Vision:** Lightweight, easy to control
- **Honda Air Blade:** Sporty design, powerful engine
- **Honda Click:** Fuel-efficient, suitable for the city
- **Yamaha Mio:** Compact, easy to maneuver in heavy traffic

Note: Automatic scooters usually have higher rental prices than manual motorcycles due to their popularity.

### 50cc Motorcycle - Choice for Foreign Tourists

If you are a foreign tourist and only have a 50cc driver's license, this type of motorcycle is a suitable choice:

- **Honda Cub 50cc:** Small engine, easy to control
- **50cc scooter:** Convenient with upright riding position

Advantages of 50cc motorcycles:
- No driver's license required (for foreign tourists under Vietnamese law)
- Maximum fuel savings
- Suitable for short trips

However, note that 50cc motorcycles have low power and are not suitable for long distances or hilly terrain.

### Electric Motorcycles and Electric Scooters - Green and Economical

If you care about the environment or want to save on fuel costs, electric vehicles are an excellent choice:

- **Electric motorcycle:** Fast speed, long distance
- **Electric scooter:** Lightweight, suitable for short distances

Advantages:
- Zero emissions, environmentally friendly
- Low operating costs
- Low noise

Disadvantages:
- Need frequent charging
- Limited travel distance
- Few public charging points

## Inspect the Motorcycle Before Accepting

This is the **most important** step before you accept the motorcycle. Never skip a thorough inspection, even if renting from a reputable provider.

### External Inspection

**1. Frame and Paint**
- Check if the frame is bent or broken
- Observe if the paint has serious scratches
- Take photos of the entire motorcycle from multiple angles before accepting

**2. Tires**
- Check tire pressure (properly inflated)
- Observe if tires are worn or cracked
- Ensure tires are not punctured

**3. Lights and Signals**
- Check headlights and low beam
- Check left and right turn signals
- Check brake lights
- Check tail lights

**4. Mirrors**
- Ensure mirrors are intact
- Check if viewing angles are clear

### Internal Inspection

**1. Dashboard**
- Check speedometer, fuel gauge
- Ensure all dashboard indicators work normally

**2. Brakes**
- Test front and rear brakes
- Brakes should not be tight or soft
- Brakes should have appropriate sensitivity

**3. Horn**
- Check if the horn works
- Horn sound should be loud enough

**4. Engine Start**
- Start the motorcycle and listen to the engine sound
- Engine should run stably, without strange noises
- Check motorcycle vibration

**5. Throttle and Clutch**
- Throttle should operate smoothly
- Clutch (for manual motorcycles) should not be stiff
- Gradually test throttle for sensitivity

### Paperwork Check

**1. Vehicle Registration**
- Check if the vehicle registration is still valid
- Verify that the license plate matches the paperwork

**2. Insurance**
- Check if the motorcycle has insurance
- Review insurance coverage

**3. Rental Contract**
- Read all terms and conditions in the contract carefully
- Check rental period, pricing, deposit
- Confirm any additional fees (if applicable)

## Daily, Weekly, or Monthly Rental?

Choosing the rental period depends on your needs:

### Daily Rental - Flexible for Short-Term Needs

**Advantages:**
- Flexible, can rent anytime
- Suitable for short trips
- No long-term commitment

**Disadvantages:**
- Highest rental price (charged per day)
- Must return on time

**When to choose daily rental:**
- You only need a motorcycle for 1-2 days
- You are unsure about the usage time
- You want to try before deciding on long-term rental

**Advantages:**

**Disadvantages:**

**When to choose weekly rental:**

- You want to save costs

## Notes When Riding in Hanoi

Hanoi has a complex traffic system, especially in the Old Quarter area. Here are important notes:

### Traffic Laws

- **Ride on the right:** In Vietnam, vehicles travel on the right side of the road
- **Traffic lights:** Absolutely obey traffic signals
- **Road markings:** Pay attention to road markings, especially yellow lines (no parking)
- **Signs:** Observe traffic signs carefully

### Traffic Safety

- **Wear a helmet:** Mandatory for road safety
- **Speed:** Follow speed limits (usually 40-50 km/h in the city)
- **Distance:** Maintain a safe distance from the vehicle in front
- **Railway:** Be careful when crossing railways, especially in Long Bien

### Hanoi Traffic Characteristics

- **High motorcycle density:** Hanoi has a very high density of motorcycles, especially during rush hour
- **Narrow streets:** Many narrow alleys and streets are difficult to navigate
- **Buses:** Be careful with buses, they often move fast
- **Trucks:** Stay away from trucks and containers

### Parking

- **Parking lots:** Use guarded parking lots
- **Parking fees:** Usually 2,000 - 5,000 VND/hour
- **Safety:** Do not leave valuables on the motorcycle
- **Lock your motorcycle:** Always lock when parking, even for a few minutes
