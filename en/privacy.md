## Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Thank you for choosing Nguyen Tu Motorcycle Rental. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

### Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use our services:

- **Personal Information**: Name, phone number, email address, and other contact details you provide when making a booking
- **Rental Information**: Rental dates, motorcycle type, pickup/delivery location
- **Payment Information**: We do not store your payment card details; payment is processed directly between you and our payment provider
- **Communication Data**: Records of our conversations via phone, Zalo, WhatsApp, or email

### How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect or receive:

- To provide, maintain, and improve our rental services
- To process your rental bookings and manage your account
- To communicate with you about your booking and provide customer support
- To send you important information about your rental and our services
- To respond to your inquiries and requests

### How We Share Your Information

We do not sell or trade your personal information. We may share your information:

- With your consent
- To comply with legal obligations
- To protect the safety and rights of our company, customers, or others

### Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

### Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

- The right to access and obtain a copy of your personal information
- The right to request correction of inaccurate information
- The right to request deletion of your personal information
- The right to restrict or object to the processing of your information
